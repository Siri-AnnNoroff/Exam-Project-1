Ordering

How to order

  1. Browse our products and select the item you want.
  2. Choose your size, colour or quantity, then click “add to cart”.
  3. Open your cart to review your items, then click “proceed to checkout.
  4. Enter your shipping address and payment option.
  5. Review your order and click “complete purchase”.

You will receive a confirmation email with your order number once your order is placed.

PAYMENT

We accept major credit and debit cards and other payment methods shown at checkout. Your card is only charged when your order is confirmed.

SHIPPING

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days, and express delivery takes 1–3 business days. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and the size of your order. You will receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

CHANGING OR CANCELLING AN ORDER

If you need to change or cancel your order, contact us at [email address] as soon as possible. We can only make changes before your order has shipped.
