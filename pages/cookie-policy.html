Cookie policy

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work properly and remember your preferences.

Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: needed for the website to function, for example keeping items in your shopping cart and keeping you logged in.
  • Preference cookies: remember your settings, such as your language or region.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it.
  • Marketing cookies: used to show you relevant ads and measure how well they work.

Managing cookies

When you first visit our site, you can accept or decline non-essential cookies using our cookie banner. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Please note that blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site, such as the checkout, from working.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
