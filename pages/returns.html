Returns

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If you are not, you can return most items within 30 days of delivery.

RETURN CONDITIONS

  • Items must be unused, unworn and in their original packaging.
  • You must include your order number or proof of purchase.
  • Sale items, gift cards and personalised products cannot be returned.

HOW TO RETURN AN ITEM

  1. Email us with your order number and the reason for the return.
  2. We will send you a return address and instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely and send it back to us.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Original shipping costs are non-refundable, and you are responsible for the cost of returning the item unless it arrived damaged or incorrect.

DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ITEMS

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and we will send a replacement or a full refund.
